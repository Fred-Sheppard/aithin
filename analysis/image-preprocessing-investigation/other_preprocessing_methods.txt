Other preprocessing methods tested

- Autocontrast (selected method): Stretched the grayscale intensity range while ignoring 1% of the darkest and lightest pixels. It improved results for the augmented model on the handwritten evaluation images, though effects varied by model and sample group.
- Full-image Lanczos resize: Resized the whole image using a smoother alternative to nearest-neighbour interpolation.
- Centered crop with Lanczos resize: Cropped the centre to make the character larger, then resized it with Lanczos.
- CLAHE: Adjusted contrast locally across small regions of the image.
- Otsu thresholding: Used one global threshold to convert the grayscale image into foreground and background pixels.
- Adaptive Gaussian thresholding: Calculated thresholds from local image regions to account for uneven backgrounds.
- Sauvola thresholding: Used local thresholds, adapting them to local brightness and contrast.
